Sethster Privacy Policy

Effective October 23, 2026

Sethster is a private app for listening to one family's collection of classical and opera recordings. It is available only by invitation to friends and family, through Apple's TestFlight. This page explains what the app and its server keep about you, and how to remove it.

What we keep

  • Your account: the username and display name you choose, and your password stored only as a one-way scrambled hash (no one, including the owner, can read your password).
  • Your account status: whether the owner has approved your request, and when.
  • Sign-in sessions: a random sign-in key for each device you use, with the date it was created and last used, so you stay signed in.
  • Your Rotation: the list of recordings you add to Heavy Rotation, in your order.
  • Answers you give: if you answer questions about recordings in the app, your answers.
  • Notification address: if you allow notifications, the push token Apple gives your device, used only to tell you when your request is approved (and to tell the owner about new requests).

What we don't keep

  • No email address, phone number, contacts, location, or payment details.
  • No advertising, analytics, or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared with anyone.
  • Your network address is used briefly, in memory only, to slow down repeated failed sign-in attempts. It is not stored with your account.

Where it lives

Everything is stored on the family's own server at home. Push notifications pass through Apple's notification service. TestFlight is run by Apple, and Apple's own privacy policy covers what TestFlight collects (such as crash reports and the feedback you choose to send).

Recordings on your phone

Recordings you download for Heavy Rotation are stored on your phone. If your access is removed, they stay playable until your phone next reaches the server, and then they are removed.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

Your information is kept while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete Account, or from the waiting screen if you haven't been approved yet). That immediately deletes your account, your sign-in sessions, your Rotation and your notification address from the server, and removes your downloads and sign-in from that phone. If the owner turns down your request, you can still sign in and delete your account the same way.

Children

Sethster is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly keep information about children under 13.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new date. For questions or to ask for your information to be deleted, contact the person who invited you, or write to contact@example.com.
